Getting here from the airport

24 hours - EUR 5.10

Airport Express bus (line 203)

No transfers. Departs from all 4 terminals and stops at Plaza de Cibeles, a short walk from the hostal. About 35-40 minutes.

2 changes - about EUR 5

Metro to Gran Via

Line 8 to Nuevos Ministerios, change to Line 10 (direction Puerta del Sur) to Tribunal, then change to Line 1 to Gran Via - the metro station closest to the hostal, just a 3-minute walk away.

Fixed fare - EUR 33

Official taxi

Flat rate to anywhere inside the M-30 ring road. Comfortable if you are travelling with luggage or family.

Prices and times are indicative and may change. Check the official Metro de Madrid and EMT websites before you travel.

For more than 40 years

A family-run hostal, in the Madrid that never changes

Hostal Prada has been opening its doors in the very centre of Madrid, just steps from Gran Via, for more than four decades. Generations of travellers have passed through here on their way to a holiday, their studies, or a work trip.

We keep what never goes out of style: comfortable rooms, a fair price, and a warm welcome that makes every guest feel at home, whether staying one night or a whole week.

We are open most of the year: we close only in August, so we can rest too.

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in starts at 2:00 PM and check-out must be no later than 12:00 PM (noon). Our front desk is open 24 hours for anything else you need.

Is there a lift?

Yes, the building has a lift.

Are pets allowed?

No, we do not currently allow pets at the hostal.

Can I leave my luggage if I arrive early or leave later?

Yes, we offer a luggage storage service, whether you arrive before check-in or your flight leaves later in the day.

Does Wi-Fi work in every room?

Yes, it is free throughout the hostal. As a complimentary service, we cannot guarantee speed or continuous availability.

Are you open all year?

We close during the month of August for our own break. The rest of the year we are open as usual.
